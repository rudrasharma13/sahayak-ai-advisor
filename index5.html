🏛️ Government Loan & Subsidy Schemes

PMEGP Scheme

Prime Minister’s Employment Generation Programme. Up to 35% subsidy for establishing new micro-enterprises in rural setups.

MUDRA Yojana

Collateral-free micro loans up to ₹10 Lakhs categorized under Shishu, Kishor, and Tarun tiers for small businesses.

PM Vishwakarma

Financial assistance, advanced skill training, toolkit incentives, and low-interest credit support for traditional artisans.

Stand-Up India

Bank loans ranging between ₹10 Lakhs and ₹1 Crore specifically designed for greenfield ventures by SC, ST, and women entrepreneurs.

PMFME Scheme

Financial, technical, and business upgrade support with credit-linked subsidies for micro food processing units.

Agriculture Infra Fund (AIF)

Medium to long-term debt financing facility for investments in post-harvest management infrastructure and community farming assets.

Kisan Credit Card (KCC)

Timely and adequate credit support to farmers for cultivation expenses, crop insurance, and allied agricultural activities.

SVAMITVA Scheme

Providing rural property owners with official ownership records (Property Cards) to leverage property for bank loans.
